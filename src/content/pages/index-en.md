I am an Assistant Professor at the [Department of Computer Systems and Technologies](https://www.uni-ruse.bg/departments/KST) at the [University of Ruse](https://www.uni-ruse.bg/), where I teach and conduct research in intelligent systems and applied artificial intelligence. With a background in programming and software development, I enjoy building practical systems that bridge research and real-world applications.

My work focuses on machine learning, computer vision, data science, and intelligent systems for energy and educational applications. I am also a strong supporter of open science and open-source software, with a belief that accessible, reproducible research helps knowledge grow through collaboration and education.
