AIGUIDE is an Erasmus+ KA2 project focused on the use of artificial intelligence for green understanding, insight generation, and decision empowerment. The project supports international collaboration in higher education and promotes AI-driven approaches for sustainability, environmental awareness, and informed decision-making.
