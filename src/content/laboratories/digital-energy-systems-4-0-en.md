I am a part of a research group, whose main goal is to develop methods for optimizing hybrid renewable energy systems in the residential sector.

### Research themes

- Optimizing energy processes across system configurations, including inverters and microinverters.
- Studying how operating conditions such as soiling and shading affect the energy efficiency of generation sources.
- Optimizing energy storage operation based on load profiles, geographic location, installed capacity, and energy exchange.
- Assessing the environmental impact of systems in the residential sector.
- Applying the Internet of Things (IoT) in energy systems.
- Organizing, storing, and analyzing big data in digital energy systems.
